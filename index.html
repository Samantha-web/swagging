How to Use the Calculator

  1. Enter the order quantity in the "Order Quantity" field.
  2. Select the working hours per day from the dropdown menu.
  3. Check the boxes under "Items to Do" for the parts you want to produce.
  4. The table will automatically calculate the quantity, total hours, and number of days required for each selected item.
  5. View the production summary by part of frame and by machine, including machine functions, total hours, and days.
  6. The machine schedule shows the start date (today) and end date for each machine based on the calculated days.
  7. Click "Reset All" to clear all inputs and selections.
